For teachers

A short guide to using the site in a lesson: what is on it, how to give students an exact link and what to do about shared computers.

How it works

  • Free and with no sign-up: students simply open a link.
  • Works in any modern browser on a computer or tablet.
  • Progress is saved in the browser on that device.
  • The site doesn’t collect student data; see the “Privacy” page for details.
  • The site comes in Ukrainian and English: there is a language switch in the header of every page. “Drive the Robot!”, “Galaxy Code”, “Mars Rover Maze” and “Cyber Shield Agents” are available in English; the rebuses are Ukrainian only for now. Progress in the games is shared between the two languages.

Direct links for students

To get the whole class onto the same task at once, give students a ready-made link. Every example below can be copied as is.

  • Robot: #level-name opens that level even if the earlier ones aren’t finished. For example, level 6 (“Loop magic”): https://otianoleksij.github.io/geek-informatic/en/games/robot/#loop-magic. Level names: first-step, turn, around-rocks, tricky-path, stone-corridor, loop-magic, staircase, loop-combo, maze, crystal-mountain. Old number-based links (#level-6) still work.
  • Galaxy Code: #level-name opens that level even if the earlier ones aren’t finished. For example, level 10 (“Perimeter”): https://otianoleksij.github.io/geek-informatic/en/games/kod-galaktyky/#perimeter. Level names: first-crystal, left-and-right, asteroid-maze, long-road, ladder, around-the-rock, every-other-cell, no-right-turn, tight-hangar, perimeter, zigzag, pinwheel, two-rings, grid, station-heart. Number-based links (#level-10) also work.
  • Rebuses (Ukrainian only): #r017 opens a specific rebus: https://otianoleksij.github.io/geek-informatic/puzzles/rebusy/#r017.
  • Mars Rover: #maze-number gives the whole class the same maze: https://otianoleksij.github.io/geek-informatic/en/games/mars/#maze-12345. The number appears in the address bar after you press “New maze”.
  • Cyber Shield Agents: there are no direct links to missions; they open in order.
  • Catalog on the home page: ?grade=7 shows activities for grade 7: https://otianoleksij.github.io/geek-informatic/en/?grade=7. type, topic and level (difficulty 1–3) work too.

Lesson tips

  • Shared computers. At the end of the lesson, ask students to clear their data on the “Privacy” page so the next class starts from scratch.
  • CyberShield ID card. The name and grade aren’t saved, so students enter them while the page is open. The ID card can be shown on screen, captured in a screenshot, or the result line can be copied and sent to the teacher.
  • Keyboard. The block editors (Galaxy Code, Mars Rover) have a “Keyboard controls” help section for students who find the mouse awkward.
  • Same conditions for everyone. Share one class-wide link to a level or a maze so that solutions can be compared.
