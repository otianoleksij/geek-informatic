KEYBOARD

Space Typing Trainer

Type the text: every correct key fires your ship's laser at the asteroid. The longer your streak without mistakes, the stronger the shots. No need to hurry, the asteroid will wait for you.

A keyboard is needed. This trainer works with the physical keyboard of a computer or laptop. You can't type on a phone or tablet without a keyboard.

Levels

Levels unlock one by one: finish a level to open the next.

    Game screen: your ship is at the bottom left, an asteroid flies in from the top right. Mistakes bring the asteroid closer.
